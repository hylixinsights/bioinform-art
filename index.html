About

Where biological data becomes art.

Every piece here is drawn from the field itself — phylogenies, genomes, embeddings, expression matrices — or made with the tools that read them. The instruments are the same. Only the intent has changed.

The idea

A pipeline ends. A picture begins.

Bioinformatics spends its days turning the living world into arrays — reads, trees, coordinates, p-values. Somewhere between the raw signal and the final figure, the data starts to look like something. We collect those moments: the frame where an alignment becomes a landscape, where a genome curls into a wheel, where ten thousand cells settle into a cloud.

Nothing here is decoration. Each piece is the honest output of a method, chosen for its beauty and shown at gallery scale — with the article, the tool, and the coordinates kept on the wall label, in plain sight.

What qualifies

Scientific Art — an image derived from real data. A source article link is required (journal URL or DOI) so the work can be traced to its evidence.

Inspired Art — an image made with bioinformatics tools but not bound to a dataset. Name the tool or method; a GitHub repo is optional but encouraged.

Image specs

  • PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG · up to 20 MB
  • Longest edge ≥ 1600 px for a crisp gallery print
  • No embedded UI, watermarks or logos

Attribution & licensing

  • You must hold the rights, or the data must be openly licensed
  • Cite the dataset / article for Scientific Art
  • Pieces are shown under CC BY 4.0 unless you note otherwise

Sharing code

If your piece is reproducible, link the repository or notebook. A README with the exact command that made the image is the gold standard.
